### `.ai/decisions/render-nav-pages-always-on.md` (new)

One decision per file, the why + what was rejected (`.ai/README.md`). Contents:

- Title + date
- **Decision**: render the home page and every page in `navTree` with the browser on every run,
  no flag; deeper pages keep today's plain fetch with Playwright only as fallback.
- **Why**: the recurring gaps in rebuilds are missing before/after screenshots and no real brand
  colours/fonts on client-rendered (Wix-style) sites — signals only a rendered page can give.
- **Rejected**: an opt-in `--screenshots` flag (gets forgotten, so the pitch lacks the
  before/after); rendering every crawled page (too slow and too rude at the 1 req/s default with
  a 500-page ceiling).
- **Degrade rule**: Playwright missing (optional dependency) or a page timing out skips that
  page's screenshot/style sample, records `error` in `screenshots/index.json` and
  `fetch-log.jsonl`, and the run exits `0`.
- **Consequences**: one shared browser instance per run (`renderWithPlaywright` returns an
  object, not a bare HTML string); aggregation and normalization are pure functions tested
  without a browser; every capture is logged with `method: "screenshot"` (guardrail).
